M.Tech project · ongoing

Residual stress and distortion in TIG-welded stainless steel joints

Experiments and modelling of what welding leaves behind inside the metal.

Material
SS316LN
Craft
TIG welding · thermo-mechanical FEA · NDT
Detail
119,743 nodes · 108,000 C3D8R elements
Tools
ABAQUS (user subroutine) · Python
Atelier
IIT Madras × IGCAR · Prof. S. Hiremath, David Vijayanand V
Period
Aug 2026 to present
Full details
  • Worked on implementing the Goldak double-ellipsoidal moving heat source model using a user-defined subroutine in ABAQUS to simulate transient heat input during TIG welding.
  • Developed a 3D thermo-mechanical (multi-physics) FEA model in ABAQUS comprising 119,743 nodes and 108,000 C3D8R solid elements to investigate welding-induced residual stresses and distortion.
  • Conducting TIG welding experiments on SS316LN, assessing residual stresses and distortion using NDT techniques.
  • Aiming to develop machine-learning surrogate models using Python to predict welding responses, reduce computational time and simulation costs, and achieve high predictive accuracy compared with conventional FEA simulations.
